HOW THE CONSEQUENCES PROPAGATE

Timing changes starts. Capacity changes flow.

  1. A new group reserves its whole assumed acid dose before acidification.
  2. After 7 assumed days, output ramps over 7 days. Existing production's sustaining acid is protected outside the boundary.
  3. Area IX limits throttle flow. Shared drying and WIP headroom then pace actual extraction; withheld potential U stays underground.

The default end-of-day WIP target is one daily drying capacity. It is an illustrative operating policy, not a measured Inkai storage capacity. Sensitivity below checks other targets.

Data provenance, boundary and primary sources

Public facts: MPP, Sat-1, Sat-2; IX 2.7 / 6.3 / 4.5 Mlb U₃O₈/year; drying 8.3 Mlb/year; approximately 98% plant recovery. Demonstrated annual capacities are converted to uniform daily limits, an assumption.

Synthetic: nine groups and their footprints, nominal potential rates, uranium inventories, commissioning stocks, delivery quantities and schedules. Assumed: acid doses, 7-day acidification, 7-day ramp, 100 mg U/L, proportional pacing, one-day WIP target, protected sustaining acid, no toll processing. Derived: kg U/day using 2.5998 lb U₃O₈/kg U and 365 days. Simulated: all states, dates, stocks, flows, products, projections and sensitivity results.

The timing mechanism is source-informed. The exercise does not reconstruct 2024, estimate real losses or calibrate block response. Nominal potential rates can exceed shared processing capacity; actual extraction is paced. Satellite recovered-U transfer is aggregated. Chemistry, hydraulic constraints and actual storage geometry are omitted.
