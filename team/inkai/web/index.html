How the assumption works

The whole acid dose is reserved at acidification start. After 7 days, production ramps over 7 days. Existing groups' acid supply is protected outside this model.

IX limits throttle solution extraction. Recovered uranium waits for drying; it is not lost when packaging is full.

Data provenance, model boundary and sources

Public facts: MPP, Sat-1, Sat-2; IX capacities 2.7 / 6.3 / 4.5 Mlb U₃O₈/year; drying 8.3 Mlb/year; approximately 98% plant recovery. Annual capacities are converted to uniform daily model limits, an assumption.

Synthetic: nine blocks and their geometry, inventories, rates, commissioning-only acid tanks, day-3/day-60 deliveries and planned starts on days 5/18. Assumptions: 7-day acidification, 7-day ramp, fixed 100 mg U/L, proportionate area throttling, no toll processing. Derived: kg U/day from annual U₃O₈ limits using 2.5998 lb U₃O₈/kg U and 365 days. Simulated: every stock, lifecycle state, commissioning date, product value and plotted trajectory.

The timing mechanism is source-informed; these values do not reconstruct the 2024 disruption. Uranium recovery behaviour is not calibrated. No ongoing-production acid response, chemistry, transport, optimization or uncertainty forecast is modelled.
